## 1. Executive summary

1. **The dominant, documented failure of Indian voice automation is not "no bot", it is "bot that cannot hear the customer".** Every independent datapoint says the same thing: code-switched Hinglish raises WER 30–50% relative ([Deepgram](https://deepgram.com/learn/hinglish-voice-ai-speech-recognition)); Whisper lands at ~16% WER on clean adult Hinglish ([HiACC, PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12329218/)); on real rural Hindi telephony even IndicWav2Vec degrades to 40.94% WER and the *same* model swings from 4% to 44% WER across districts ([Gnani rural-ASR paper](https://www.gnani.ai/resources/research/the-rural-india-asr-problem-engineering-speech-recognition-for-background-noise)); an end-to-end voice form-filling pilot for ARMMAN mothers lost up to ~41 points of form completion when the LLM received real ASR transcripts instead of reference text ([FormBharo, arXiv 2608.06027](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06027)). Gnani's own public example of the business consequence is "teen hazaar rupay ka payment Friday tak" being written to the CRM as "teen rupay" ([Gnani Hinglish blog](https://www.gnani.ai/resources/blogs/blog-code-switching-speech-recognition-hinglish-asr)). **Numbers, dates and amounts spoken in mixed Hindi/English on 8 kHz lines are the single most valuable thing to get right**, and that is exactly Prisma's ITN + 8 kHz pitch.
2. **Regulators are manufacturing demand for "prove what was said on the call".** RBI's Digital Lending Directions (8 May 2025) and the 2026 recovery-agent amendment (effective 1 Jan 2027) require recorded, logged, 08:00–19:00 collection calls with identity and grievance disclosure and six-month retention ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/privacy-protection/1709322/rbi-introduced-digital-lending-direction-2025), [CorpLawUpdates](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/rbi-amendment-directions-recovery-agents-loan-dues-2026)). IRDAI logged 26,667 "unfair business practice" (mis-selling) complaints against life insurers in FY25, up 14% ([Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/business/mis-selling-complaints-rise-against-life-insurers-in-fy25-1927349)), is proposing commission clawbacks ([Toolyt](https://toolyt.com/pulse/insurance/irdai-mis-selling-clawback-public-records-analysis)), and has long "encouraged" pre-issuance verification calls ([IRDAI PIVC circular, 12 May 2020](https://irdai.gov.in/document-detail?documentId=394397)). DPDP Rules were notified 14 Nov 2025 with substantive obligations from May 2027 and notices available in any Eighth-Schedule language on request ([PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf)). Manual QA covers 1–5% of calls ([Mihup](https://mihup.ai/blog/contact-center-ai-india-multilingual-solution), [Gnani blog](https://www.gnani.ai/resources/blogs/speech-to-text-contact-center-automation-india)); the other 95%+ is invisible to compliance, and Hindi portions are invisible to English-only monitors ([Qualia Bits](https://qualiabits.com/blog/dpdp-call-qa-compliance-india/)).
3. **Public helplines are drowning and are already going "voice bot", but mostly in Hindi/English only.** 112 takes ~8 lakh calls/day ([helpqr](https://helpqr.org/blog/sos-app-india)), 108 ~80,000/day ([ZHL](https://zhl.org.in/emergency-108-ambulance)), Tele-MANAS 12.33 lakh calls in 2025 in 20 languages with ~600 counsellors ([IMPRI](https://www.impriindia.com/insights/policy-update/tele-manas-indias-national-tele-mental-health-programme/)), Kisan Call Centres 35.22 lakh calls in 2022-23 of which 1.69 lakh went unanswered and volumes are falling year on year ([Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/national/govt-to-revamp-kisan-call-centers-with-advanced-communication-technologies-amid-decline-in-calls-news-307342)), 1930 cyber helpline ~10,000 calls/day officially ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?NoteId=155384&ModuleId=3&reg=48&lang=2)) with victims reporting 20 failed dial attempts in a day ([Grapevine](https://www.grapevine.in/post/1930-cyber-crime-helpline-is-a-joke-got-more-scam-calls-after-reporting-a-fraud-d921be72-4b5b-46c3-988d-8d77384a2eb1)). The Centre launched the CPGRAMS voice bot "Samadhan Didi" on Bhashini on 30 May 2026 ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2266985&reg=3&lang=1)), so "government voice bot" is no longer novel; **the gap is doing it on noisy 8 kHz calls with correct numbers, not doing it at all.**
4. **Buyers in 2025-26 ask for five things:** (a) regional languages beyond Hindi/English (Meesho's bot handles 60,000 calls/day but only in Hindi+English and promised six more languages — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/26/ai-helps-indias-meesho-cut-customer-call-costs-by-75)); (b) cost per resolved contact in the ₹12–29 range against ₹40–120 for a human **[VENDOR]** ([Caller Digital](https://www.caller.digital/blog/ai-voice-agent-vs-human-india-cost-roi)); (c) audit trails and data residency, with air-gapped operation appearing as pass/fail gates in government and defence tenders **[VENDOR]** ([Caller Digital](https://caller.digital/blog/voice-ai-data-residency-sovereignty-india-dpdp-2026); Indian Bank's own DPDP RFP on GeM: [PDF](https://indianbank.bank.in/documents/20117/518589/IBDPDPRFP2026_07_23_14_17_252026_08_14_14_40_01.pdf/485c3ed6-0326-56a8-a4b4-b84b863e6ff8)); (d) human handoff that actually works (the recurring Reddit complaint is bank voice assistants that refuse to connect to a person — reported via search summary, no thread URL retrievable); (e) explainable AI with a human override path, now formalised by RBI's FREE-AI report of 13 Aug 2025 ([Law.asia](https://law.asia/rbi-ai-framework/)).
5. **What maps onto Gnani, in order of defensibility:** 8 kHz noisy-telephony STT (Gramvaani 24.94% WER vs Azure 29.88 and Deepgram 35.20 — Gnani's own benchmark), code-mixed Hinglish with ITN for ₹/lakh/dates, two-speaker diarized batch for compliance review, 8 kHz mulaw/alaw TTS so prompts sound native on PSTN, and self-hosted Evon for the "no transcript leaves the premises" story. **Three seeds stand out (Section 5): the PIVC/mis-selling verifier, the collections "promise-to-pay ledger", and the elder scam-shield.** The first two ride a live regulatory deadline; the third rides the most emotionally shareable news cycle in India right now (digital-arrest frauds at ₹2,000–3,000 crore/year).

## 5. Idea seeds

Scoring key for awards (from the brief): **RL** regional language · **CM** code-mixed Hinglish/Tanglish/Benglish · **RI** real-world impact · **NT** noisy telephony · **CR** creativity · **D60** 60-second demo. Build constraint reminder: Evon must be self-hosted (rent a cloud GPU for demo days, or document a stand-in; see `05-models.md`), batch STT has no ITN and max 2 speakers, realtime WS is one language per session.

### S1. **Vaada** — promise-to-pay ledger and Fair-Practices audit for small NBFC collections *(top-3)*
- **Situation:** P1 + P2. Collections teams churn ~100%/yr, PTPs are lost between telecaller CRM and field app, recovery complaints are in RBI's top 5 every year, and from 1 Jan 2027 every recovery call must be recorded and retained 6 months within 08:00–19:00.
- **Gnani models:** Prisma realtime WS at 8 kHz for the live call (Hinglish, `format=transcribe` for ₹/dates, `bias_list` for product/branch names) → Evon extracts `{amount, date, mode, objections}` and checks the FPC script (entity named? grievance channel stated? abusive words? call time within window?) → Timbre reads the PTP back for borrower confirmation (consent captured as spoken "haan", logged with DPDP notice version) → Prisma batch diarized (2 speakers) over the archive for after-the-fact QA.
- **Awards:** CM, NT, RI, D60. Gnani's home turf, so "fit" is maximal.
- **60-s scene:** Phone-quality Hinglish clip plays ("Sir teen hazaar Friday tak kar dunga, baaki agle mahine"). Left pane: live transcript with ₹3,000 and date rendered by ITN; right pane: PTP card auto-filled, three green compliance ticks and one red flag ("call placed at 19:42 — outside RBI window"). Cut to a Tamil borrower, same flow. End card: "written to the ledger, nothing left the box".
- **Biggest risk:** It is Gnani's own Collect365/Aura365 territory and other entrants (IndicVoiceCare, Bolo) are adjacent. Mitigation: aim at the *sub-₹500 Cr NBFC/MFI that cannot buy Collect365*, obsess over number/date correctness on 8 kHz, and ship the audit log, not a chatbot. **[SPEC]** judges from Gnani will recognise the problem instantly, which is a double-edged sword.

### S2. **Sahi Bikri** — regional-language pre-issuance verification and "said vs sold" diff for insurance *(top-3, my pick for strongest)*
- **Situation:** P3. 26,667 mis-selling complaints in FY25, IRDAI panels and clawback proposals, PIVC "encouraged" since 2020 but done by humans in Hindi/English on a sample; elderly Tamil/Bengali/Marathi buyers are the typical victims.
- **Gnani models:** Timbre (8 kHz mulaw, 10 languages) reads the IRDAI-style verification script with the *actual* policy facts; Prisma + ITN captures "haan / ₹24,000 / pandrah saal"; Evon compares (a) the telesales recording transcript (batch, diarized) with (b) the policy schedule and (c) the customer's PIVC answers, and emits discrepancy flags ("agent said 5-year lock-in, policy is 15"). Voice cloning is *not* used (deepfake optics).
- **Awards:** RL, CM, RI, D60; creativity moderate.
- **60-s scene:** Split screen. Left: 20-s telesales snippet in Hinglish where the agent says "paanch saal mein double". Right: Timbre calls the customer in Tamil, reads the real terms, asks three yes/no questions, customer says "illa, aiynthu varusham sonnaanga". Evon shows a red "term mismatch" card with both quotes and timestamps. End: "₹2.57 lakh grievances a year start with a sentence like this".
- **Biggest risk:** Outbound PSTN is out of scope in 5 weeks; demo via browser WebRTC resampled to 8 kHz, clearly labelled. Also low overlap with Gnani's published case studies (they do BFSI collections, not insurance verification), which cuts both ways: open niche, but less "obviously Gnani" **[SPEC]**.

### S3. **Suraksha Kavach** — on-call scam-shield for elderly parents *(top-3 for shareability)*
- **Situation:** P8. Digital-arrest frauds at ₹2,000–3,000 crore/yr, individual elderly victims losing ₹3–23 crore, callers kept on the line for hours; families ask why nobody intervened.
- **Gnani models:** Prisma realtime WS at 8 kHz on the call audio (Hindi/Marathi/Tamil, since scammers speak the victim's language) → Evon classifier for the scam grammar ("CBI", "Aadhaar misuse", "safe account", "do not tell anyone", urgency) → Timbre whispers a warning in the parent's language and SMS/calls the adult child. Optional: `bias_list` with agency names.
- **Awards:** NT, RL, RI, CR, D60. Highest viral potential on LinkedIn/X of anything here **[SPEC]**, which matters because selection is engagement-weighted.
- **60-s scene:** Grandmother's phone on speaker; a "CBI officer" in Hindi demands a transfer. Risk meter climbs as phrases land; at 70% the app interrupts in Marathi "Aai, hi fasavnuk aahe, phone theva" and the son's phone buzzes with the transcript. End card with the ₹ loss statistic.
- **Biggest risk:** Android forbids third-party call recording, so a real on-device product needs a call-screening SIP number or speakerphone capture; be honest that the demo is "second-device listening". Also false positives on legitimate bank calls; keep a human-in-the-loop (family) rather than auto-blocking.

### S4. **Kisan Overflow** — Kisan Call Centre after-hours and busy-line voice agent
- **Situation:** P5 + P6. KCC volumes fell from 58 lakh to 35 lakh in two years with ~1.7–3.6 lakh unanswered per year; ministry promised AI; callers are on exactly the 2G/AMR-NB path Gnani's paper describes.
- **Gnani models:** Prisma 8 kHz (hi/mr/te/kn/ta/bn/gu/pa), Timbre same, Evon RAG over the public KCC query–answer corpus (data.gov.in) plus Agmarknet prices; `with_denoise` on batch for logging.
- **Awards:** RL, NT, RI ("small town"), D60.
- **60-s scene:** ffmpeg-simulated 2G call with tractor noise; a Marathi farmer asks about "soyabean var pivla mosaic". Answer in Marathi in 4 s, then "tumchya talukyat aaj soyabean bhav ₹4,350". Side panel shows the retrieved KCC Q&A and the WER-preserving denoise step.
- **Biggest risk:** Hallucinated agronomy advice; mitigate with retrieval-only answers plus "FTA callback" fallback. Evon hosting cost/latency on a rented GPU. Samadhan Didi makes "govt voice bot" less novel.

### S5. **Dobhashi Rider** — live rider↔customer interpreter for last-mile delivery
- **Situation:** P11. 20–30% first-attempt failures, "customer not available"/"address not found" dominate, Bengaluru rider–customer language friction, Hindi-only calls miss the South.
- **Gnani models:** Two Prisma WS sessions (rider's language, customer's language), Evon translation + address normalisation (landmarks, flat numbers via ITN), Timbre in both languages at 8 kHz; `bias_list` seeded with locality names from the pincode.
- **Awards:** RL, CM, CR, NT, D60.
- **60-s scene:** Rider in Hindi: "Madam, main gate pe hoon, teesra floor?" → customer hears Kannada; replies in Kannada → rider hears Hindi with the address card updating ("3rd floor, blue gate, opposite Ganesha temple").
- **Biggest risk:** Latency chain (STT → LLM → TTS twice) on a phone call; mid-call language switching breaks the one-language WS session. Logistics companies (Delhivery) already partner with Gnani in The Ken competition, so expect comparable entries **[SPEC]**.

### S6. **Peechhe Kya Hua** — the "other 98%" Hinglish call-QA for a telesales/collections floor
- **Situation:** P4 + P2. QA samples 1–5%; English-only monitors miss Hindi disclosures; compliance phrase-matching needs <8% WER.
- **Gnani models:** Prisma batch with diarization and `with_denoise`, `multi_lang_codes`, Evon scoring against a rubric (disclosures, consent, prohibited phrases, empathy), dashboard of per-agent and per-language error rates.
- **Awards:** CM, NT, RI.
- **60-s scene:** Drop a ZIP of 50 calls; watch the coverage bar go 3% → 100%; click one red call to hear the 8 s where the agent skipped the grievance disclosure in Hindi.
- **Biggest risk:** It *looks like* the "audio-notes summariser" take-home that ~30 repos already built (see `06` §2.1). Only worth doing as the back-office of S1 or S2, not standalone.

### S7. **Dhokha Desk** — multilingual fraud-intake assistant for bank fraud desks (not for 1930 itself)
- **Situation:** P7. 1930 lines unreachable in the golden hour; intake needs UTRs, amounts, timestamps, bank names from a panicking caller.
- **Gnani models:** Prisma 8 kHz + ITN (₹ amounts, dates, 12-digit UTR via `transcribe`), `bias_list` of bank/UPI app names, Timbre read-back of every digit group for confirmation, Evon fills a structured complaint (NCRP-shaped) and drafts the SMS.
- **Awards:** RL, NT, RI, D60.
- **60-s scene:** Bengali caller on a bad line; UTR read back digit-by-digit in Bengali; one correction; complaint PDF generated with timestamps.
- **Biggest risk:** Must not impersonate 1930/I4C (hackathon optics and legality). Long digit strings on 8 kHz are the hardest ASR case — measure it first (gate: ≥90% exact UTR capture on a 30-clip test set) **[SPEC]**.

### S8. **Kilkari Do-Tarafa** — two-way maternal follow-up with validation
- **Situation:** P10. Kilkari is one-way, 50% first-attempt pickup, under half listen to half the content; FormBharo shows ASR errors wipe ~41 points of completion unless rule-based validation is layered in.
- **Gnani models:** Prisma 8 kHz Hindi/Marathi with ITN for dates and weights, Evon extraction + hard validators (date ranges, kg bounds), Timbre read-back.
- **Awards:** RL, NT, RI.
- **60-s scene:** A mother answers in Bhojpuri-flavoured Hindi; the bot confirms "aapka doosra tika 12 tareekh ko hai?" and logs the ANC visit date; validation catches an impossible weight and re-asks.
- **Biggest risk:** Health-adjacent claims and ethics; keep strictly to scheduling/data capture. ARMMAN/academics own the space.

### S9. **Vasooli Awaaz** — voice ledger for MFI field officers in noisy villages
- **Situation:** P14. Cash collections keyed by hand; Vasool already sells voice entry; CreditAccess Grameen is a Gnani customer.
- **Gnani models:** Prisma REST ≤60 s clips with ITN, `bias_list` of the day's borrower names, Timbre read-back; Evon reconciles against the route sheet.
- **Awards:** NT, RL ("small town"), RI.
- **60-s scene:** Officer in a courtyard with goats and a pump running says "Sunita Devi, baarah sau pachaas, cash"; ledger row appears; read-back in Hindi.
- **Biggest risk:** Low novelty; direct competitor exists. Good as a secondary module of S1.

### S10. **Mandi Bhav Line** — dialect-tolerant commodity price and arrival line
- **Situation:** P5/P6. Farmers want today's price in their mandi; KCC is where they ask; prices live on Agmarknet; the call comes from a field on 2G.
- **Gnani models:** Prisma 8 kHz with `bias_list` of mandi and commodity names, Timbre with domain TN for "₹4,350 per quintal", Evon only for disambiguation.
- **Awards:** RL, NT, CR, D60.
- **60-s scene:** Three callers in Marathi, Telugu and Punjabi ask for onion/cotton/wheat prices; each hears the answer in their language in under 5 s, with the noise overlay visible.
- **Biggest risk:** Thin; judges may see a lookup bot. Fold into S4 unless you want the simplest possible high-polish demo.

### S11. **Fuzz-the-Line** — a noisy-telephony robustness harness (developer-tool angle)
- **Situation:** Gnani's own open problem: "Until WER numbers on AMR-NB compressed audio at 5.9 kbps with rural noise are published by any vendor, practitioners cannot make evidence-based decisions" ([rural paper](https://www.gnani.ai/resources/research/the-rural-india-asr-problem-engineering-speech-recognition-for-background-noise)); the challenge brief explicitly welcomes "developers creating tools that simplify AI usage".
- **Gnani models:** Prisma REST/WS as the system under test; Timbre to synthesise ground-truth utterances with amounts/dates in 10 languages; a pipeline that applies AMR-NB, packet loss, AGC, SNR 0–10 dB and reports WER/CER/ITN-exactness per condition.
- **Awards:** NT, CR, D60 (as a dashboard).
- **60-s scene:** Slider from clean → 5.9 kbps + 5 dB; watch "₹24,000" survive or die per language; export a report.
- **Biggest risk:** EULA clause on publishing benchmark results (see `06` §2); keep comparisons Gnani-vs-conditions, never Gnani-vs-competitor. Also no "real situation" face, so weak on RI and on social engagement.

### Which three and why
1. **S2 Sahi Bikri (insurance PIVC verifier)** — the problem is in the news this quarter (IRDAI Dec 2025 panels, clawback proposals, 26,667 complaints), nobody in the public entry list is on it, and it exercises *all three* public models with ITN and 8 kHz TTS in a regional language in one 60-second scene. Risk is low (no telephony needed to tell the story).
2. **S1 Vaada (collections PTP ledger)** — the most "Gnani" problem with a hard regulatory date (1 Jan 2027) and the clearest numeric payoff ("teen hazaar" vs "teen"). Risk is crowding, so differentiate on the audit ledger and the sub-scale NBFC.
3. **S3 Suraksha Kavach (elder scam-shield)** — weakest "enterprise" story but the strongest engagement story, and the selection is engagement-weighted; it is noisy-telephony and regional-language by construction. Risk is the call-audio capture caveat, which must be stated upfront.

If forced to pick one for a solo build: **S2**, with S6's batch QA as its back office (the telesales recording diff), which reuses the same Evon rubric code. S4 (Kisan Overflow) is the best alternative if the team prefers the "small town / regional language" award over BFSI.
