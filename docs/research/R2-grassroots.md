## 1. Executive summary

1. **The best-documented, money-linked voice pain in non-metro India right now is ASHA/ANM data entry.** One million ASHAs double-enter paper diaries into 7+ apps, work past midnight, pay for their own data, and in Karnataka 42,000 of them struck in Jan 2025 because a glitching RCH portal withheld incentives that are *only paid if the data is entered*. UP's 150,000 ASHAs still claim incentives on **paper vouchers** that a block manager types in. Nobody has shipped a phone-call path for this. ([New Lines](https://newlinesmag.com/reportage/indias-digital-health-push-is-overworking-its-front-line-women/), [NewsClick](https://www.newsclick.in/Karnataka-ASHA-Lose-Incentives-due-Software-Glitch), [ICOR](https://www.icor.info/en/en/2025/significant-victory-of-karnataka-asha-workers), [IHAT](https://www.ihat.in/resources/the-asha-incentive-application/))
2. **Voice already works at grassroots scale when it is a *phone call*, not an app.** Gram Vaani (100k MAU, 10k calls/day, missed-call + callback IVR, 15 years), CGNet Swara (1M+ calls, 14 years), Kisan Call Centres (35 lakh calls in 2022-23 despite a 40% decline), Kilkari/mMitra (millions of mothers), UPI 123PAY IVR in 12 languages. Apps that *added* work (LokOS, Ni-kshay, e-Shram, kirana storefronts, MDM360 Shield) stalled or were withdrawn. ([MIT Solve](https://solve.mit.edu/solutions/35346), [IGF 2024](https://intgovforum.org/en/content/igf-2024-lightning-talk-2-cgnet-swara-giving-a-voice-to-a-ravaged-neglected-region), [Outlook](https://www.outlookindia.com/national/govt-to-revamp-kisan-call-centers-with-advanced-communication-technologies-amid-decline-in-calls-news-307342))
3. **The documented killer of grassroots voice AI is ASR error on dialect + noise.** ASHABot (Rajasthan, 2024): transcription errors hit **38.3% of audio messages** and *deterred ASHAs from using voice at all*. FormBharo (ARMMAN, 2026): form-completion dropped **up to 41 points** when LLMs got real-speech transcripts instead of clean ones. Rural Bhojpuri women: **33.8% WER** vs ~15% for standard Hindi. Gnani's own paper says deployed Hindi IVRs "routinely" sit at 25-35% WER on AMR-NB. This is exactly the Noise Canceller / Main Character gap, and it is where Prisma's 8 kHz telephony training is a real, not cosmetic, advantage. ([ASHABot](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.10913), [FormBharo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06027), [Sruti](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.09653), [Gnani rural paper](https://www.gnani.ai/resources/research/the-rural-india-asr-problem-engineering-speech-recognition-for-background-noise))
4. **Farmers' pain is concentrated in four moments, all of which have a deadline or a rupee attached:** PM-Kisan/AgriStack identity mismatches (e-KYC, Aadhaar-land name mismatch, "zero match score", 7-30 day fixes), **PMFBY crop-loss intimation within 72 hours** (via app or 14447), the mandi gate (radio says MSP ₹2,425, trader pays ₹1,950, Agmarknet publishes only a modal price), and KCC renewal at a rigid bank branch. Kisan e-Mitra (Wadhwani AI + Bhashini) answers 20k PM-Kisan queries/day by voice+text, but **only on the portal/app**, and the 155261 helpline is still a DTMF IVRS. ([agristack.co.in](https://agristack.co.in/farmer-registry-challenges-fixing-name-mismatches-zero-match-scores-and-land-errors/), [cmv360](https://www.cmv360.com/news/farmers-can-get-compensation-under-pmfby-if-crop-loss-is-reported-in-time), [Arun Rajiah](https://arunrajiah.substack.com/p/the-rate-the-farmer-hears-on-the), [apolitical](https://apolitical.co/en/navigator/case-studies/kisan-e-mitra-indias-voice-enabled-ai-support-for-farmers))
5. **Kirana "voice khata" is already a crowded shelf (VoiceKhata ₹199/mo, Dukaan AI voice billing in 24 languages, PuraHisab, Khatabook itself). Avoid it.** The *unserved* kirana voice artefact is the **distributor-side** one: a beat salesman receiving dozens of Hinglish/Marathi WhatsApp voice notes ("do peti Parle-G, paanch kilo chini") and retyping them into orders. ([VoiceKhata](https://voicekhata.com/), [Dukaan AI](https://dukaanai.co.in/kirana-billing-app/), [dives.in](https://www.dives.in/article/2026-09-21-b2b-procurement-platform-for-kirana-shops))
6. **SHG/FPO group bookkeeping is the one grassroots niche where Prisma's *batch diarization* is load-bearing** (a weekly meeting is a multi-speaker recording of savings and repayments), and LokOS's documented duplicate-entry and sync problems give it a sourced villain. Nobody in the public challenge repos has touched diarization. ([Daffodil LokOS case](https://www.daffodilsw.com/case-study/digital-transformation-of-lokos-platform/), [IFPRI FPO](https://www.ifpri.org/blog/navigating-growth-challenges-strengthening-farmer-producer-organizations-in-odisha-india/))
7. **My three picks (see §4):** (A) **Claim-by-Call for ASHAs** (voice visit log + auto-filled incentive claim over an 8 kHz call), (B) **Bachat Baithak** (SHG meeting → diarized per-member ledger, read back by Timbre), (C) **72-Ghante Fasal Call** (PMFBY crop-loss intimation by noisy field call with ITN'd survey numbers, areas and dates). All three are phone-first, have a rupee or a deadline on screen, use synthetic data naturally (Timbre voices + noise + AMR-NB codec), and are not in the already-taken "form-filling / support bot / Bhojpuri" niches.

A student clone will not stand out |
| **MDM360 Shield (Haryana, 2021)** | GPS attendance app for ASHAs | 20,000+ ASHAs protested, app withdrawn; Telangana GPS protests 2025 ([New Lines](https://newlinesmag.com/reportage/indias-digital-health-push-is-overworking-its-front-line-women/)) | Anything that smells of surveillance kills trust; ASHABot users also feared monitoring ([ASHABot](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.10913)) |
| **RCH portal / ASHA-Nidhi (Karnataka)** | Incentive paid only when data is entered | Save glitch → months unpaid → 42,000 on strike (Jan 2025) → ₹10,000 fixed honorarium won ([ICOR](https://www.icor.info/en/en/2025/significant-victory-of-karnataka-asha-workers)) | Data entry *is* the pay channel; shaving minutes off it is a wage increase |
| **LokOS (NRLM)** | SHG transaction app for 10 Cr women | Peak-load slowdowns, duplicates, e-KYC name bugs ([Daffodil](https://www.daffodilsw.com/case-study/digital-transformation-of-lokos-platform/), [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.microware.cdfi&hl=en_IN)) | Capture must work offline/asynchronously (record now, sync later), which is batch STT's natural shape |
| **e-Shram (2021-)** | Informal-worker registry | 290M registered, "has not translated into any substantial new benefits", English/Hindi only, crashed often ([WIEGO](https://www.wiego.org/blog/registering-informal-workers-india-e-shram-opportunity-lost/)) | Registration without a payoff is a dead demo; show the payoff |
| **Kisan Call Centres (2004-)** | 22-language toll-free advisory | Calls fell 40% FY21→FY23; 1.69 lakh unanswered; govt promised chatbots/AI in 2023 ([Outlook](https://www.outlookindia.com/national/govt-to-revamp-kisan-call-centers-with-advanced-communication-technologies-amid-decline-in-calls-news-307342)) | Still 35 lakh calls/yr: the phone is not dead, it is under-served |
| **Avaaj Otalo (Gujarat, 2009-10)** | IVR Q&A forum for farmers | Q&A forum was the most-used feature; research pilot ended ([ACM](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1753326.1753434)) | Farmers want to *ask*, not browse; pilots without an owner die |
| **Gram Vaani / CGNet Swara (2009-)** | Missed-call IVR community media | Still running: 100k MAU, 10k calls/day, 8 min/call; 1M+ calls on Swara ([MIT Solve](https://solve.mit.edu/solutions/35346), [IGF 2024](https://intgovforum.org/en/content/igf-2024-lightning-talk-2-cgnet-swara-giving-a-voice-to-a-ravaged-neglected-region)) | The missed-call → callback pattern is the proven grassroots UX; copy it |
| **Jugalbandi (2023)** | WhatsApp voice bot for schemes | Repo renamed `schemes_chatbot_deprecated`; 10 languages/171 schemes at peak ([GitHub](https://github.com/OpenNyAI/jugalbandi_chatbot)) | Scheme-Q&A bots are a graveyard; pick a transaction, not a FAQ |
| **ASHABot (2024)** | WhatsApp LLM Q&A for ASHAs | Worked (85-96% accuracy) but 38.3% of audio had transcription errors and ASHAs stopped using voice ([ASHABot](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.10913)) | The ASR layer, not the LLM, decided voice adoption. That is Gnani's lane |
| **FormBharo (ARMMAN, 2026)** | Hindi voice form-filling for antenatal enrolment | Completion fell up to ~41 points on real transcripts; rule-based validation recovered most ([arXiv 2608.06027](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06027)) | Pair Evon with deterministic slot validation and ITN; show the confirm-by-voice loop |
| **Kilkari / mMitra** | Outbound IVR health messages | Missed calls from bad time slots; 40% dropout; bandits +12% pickup ([PMLR 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.16356)) | One-way voice is leaky; two-way voice (ask when to call back) is the obvious upgrade |
| **eNAM** | Electronic mandi trading | ~12% of arrivals traded electronically in 1,400 mandis (NITI 2024 via [IMPRI](https://www.impriindia.com/insights/policy-update/e-nam-infrastructure-and-price-discovery/)) | Price *information* by voice is cheaper and more wanted than price *transaction* |
| **Khabri** (audio platform, Hindi) | Vernacular audio content | YC lists it inactive; peaked ~6M users ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/khabri)) | Content plays need Kuku FM budgets; stay in utility |

**Patterns [SPEC]:** (1) grassroots voice survives when it is a phone call with no data plan; (2) adoption dies at the ASR layer on dialect + noise, not at the LLM; (3) tools that touch *money or a deadline* retain, FAQs do not; (4) surveillance features trigger revolt; (5) double entry is the single most-hated chore across ASHAs, SHG bookkeepers and FPO CEOs.

## 4. Idea seeds

Format: real situation → load-bearing Gnani model(s) → award fit → 60 s demo scene → biggest risk. Feasibility assumes FastAPI + Prisma WS/batch + Timbre + a small Evon endpoint (or a stand-in LLM clearly labelled, as Bolo did). **Synthetic data recipe for all seeds:** script utterances → Timbre voices (several of the 42) → mix public noise (cattle, tractor, market, rain; e.g., open noise corpora) at 0-10 dB SNR → `ffmpeg` AMR-NB at 8 kHz to simulate a 2G call → feed Prisma with `x-sample-rate 8000`. Fake names, fake 10-digit numbers starting with a non-allocated prefix, fake khasra numbers.

### ★ Seed 1: **Claim-by-Call** (ASHA voice visit log + auto incentive claim) — STRONGEST
- **Situation:** ASHAs double-enter diaries into 7+ apps until midnight; incentives are paid only when data is entered; Karnataka strike (42,000), UP paper vouchers (150,000 ASHAs, 15-day cycle). §2 rows 1-2.
- **Gnani:** Prisma realtime 8 kHz (works from a feature phone call), ITN (dates, counts, phone numbers), Evon → NHM activity codes + beneficiary record, Timbre read-back ("Teen ANC visits, ek institutional delivery, kul ₹1,350. Sahi hai?"). Hindi + Marathi/Kannada toggles hit Main Character.
- **Awards:** Real Deal (primary), Noise Canceller (field audio), Main Character (Marathi/Kannada).
- **60 s scene:** 0-10 s: ASHA diary photo + New Lines quote "we do everything twice". 10-40 s: she dials, speaks three visits over goat/traffic noise (shown as waveform + "8 kHz AMR-NB"); the register and the NHM claim form fill live; ₹ total appears. 40-55 s: Timbre reads it back, she says "haan", SMS summary. 55-60 s: "15 days → 2 minutes".
- **Biggest risk:** looks like Bolo's "voice form-filling". Mitigation: lead with *money* (incentive claim), the *phone call* path (no app, no data plan), and the Karnataka/UP evidence. Secondary risk: no real NHM integration; be explicit that output is a CSV/PDF the BCPM can upload.

### ★ Seed 2: **Bachat Baithak** (SHG meeting → diarized ledger) — STRONGEST, most technically distinctive
- **Situation:** 10 Cr SHG women; bookkeeper records weekly savings/repayments on paper then LokOS, which duplicates and loses entries. §2 row 12.
- **Gnani:** **Prisma batch with diarization** (the only grassroots niche where it is load-bearing) + ITN for ₹ amounts → per-member rows; Evon reconciles ("Sunita ne ₹200 bachat, ₹500 kisht"); Timbre reads totals back in the group's language for assent.
- **Awards:** Real Deal, Main Character (Marathi/Kannada/Telugu SHGs), Mixed Vibes if members mix Hindi-English numbers.
- **60 s scene:** 8 Timbre voices "meet" (synthetic, labelled); upload the 2-minute recording; diarization colours speakers; ledger fills member by member; mismatch flagged ("Rekha said ₹500, bookkeeper wrote ₹50"); Timbre reads totals; export sheet.
- **Biggest risk:** diarization on overlapping, noisy speech (DISPLACE-M exists precisely because this is hard). Mitigation: structured turn-taking (bookkeeper calls names) is how meetings actually run; measure and show DER on your synthetic set honestly.

### ★ Seed 3: **72-Ghante Fasal Call** (PMFBY crop-loss intimation by noisy field call) — STRONGEST for video urgency
- **Situation:** Loss must be intimated within 72 hours via app or 14447 with crop, khasra/survey no., area, cause, date. §2 row 7.
- **Gnani:** Prisma 8 kHz in rain/wind; **ITN for survey numbers, acres/bigha/guntha, dates**; Evon slot-filling with deterministic validation (FormBharo lesson); Timbre read-back; countdown clock.
- **Awards:** Real Deal, Noise Canceller (the whole point), Main Character (Marathi/Kannada/Telugu farmers).
- **60 s scene:** hailstorm footage (stock/synthetic) → farmer calls → speaks in Marathi over rain → fields fill (crop, gat no., 1.5 acre, hail, date) → Timbre confirms → "Intimation ID + 71:40:12 remaining".
- **Biggest risk:** cannot file to the real PMFBY/14447 back end; demo is a mock intimation record. Say so, and position it as a front end any insurer/CSC could adopt.

### Seed 4: **Mandi Bhav Callback** (missed call → prices spoken in your language)
- **Situation:** Farmer hears MSP on radio, sells to trader for ₹475/qtl less; Agmarknet gives modal only. §2 row 8.
- **Gnani:** Prisma hears crop + mandi in dialect; Timbre reads min/modal/max for 3 nearest mandis and the gap vs the trader's offer; Agmarknet public data (non-personal, so real data is allowed).
- **Awards:** Real Deal, Main Character. **Best fast fallback** if seeds 1-3 slip: 2-week build.
- **60 s:** feature phone, missed call, callback, voice reads prices, screen shows "trader ₹1,950 vs Latur modal ₹2,310".
- **Risk:** low novelty (Gramhal, WhatsApp rate groups, radio); Agmarknet API latency and gaps.

### Seed 5: **Beat-Salesman Voice-Note Desk** (kirana voice notes → order sheet)
- **Situation:** Distributor salesmen retype Hinglish/Marathi voice-note replenishment lists. §2 row 14.
- **Gnani:** Prisma batch on WhatsApp voice notes; code-mixed brand names + Hindi quantities (Mixed Vibes); ITN; Evon → SKU table; Timbre confirmation back to the kirana.
- **Awards:** Mixed Vibes (primary), Real Deal.
- **60 s:** 10 voice notes drop in → one consolidated order sheet with totals → a confirmation voice note goes back.
- **Risk:** crowded (KiranaOS, TarkApp); no DMS/Tally integration in 5 weeks; retail-tech is far from Gnani's BFSI/government identity.

### Seed 6: **Dilli-Bengaluru Bridge** (live kn↔hi interpretation on a delivery call)
- **Situation:** viral Kannada-vs-Hindi rider disputes, assault in Yelahanka. §2 row 15.
- **Gnani:** Prisma kn + hi streaming, Evon translation, Timbre both ways; Mixed Vibes native.
- **Awards:** Mixed Vibes, Main Character.
- **60 s:** split-screen call, each side hears their own language, address confirmed.
- **Risk:** latency of STT→LLM→TTS on a live call; politically charged framing can backfire on LinkedIn; platforms will not integrate.

### Seed 7: **PM-Kisan "Paisa Kyun Ruka" on a feature phone**
- **Situation:** instalment stuck → which of e-KYC / Aadhaar seeding / land seeding / Farmer-ID zero match. §2 row 6.
- **Gnani:** Prisma 8 kHz + Evon triage + Timbre; synthetic beneficiary states.
- **Awards:** Real Deal.
- **Risk:** Kisan e-Mitra already does this by voice (on app/web) with 95 lakh queries; a judge will ask "why not Bhashini". Medium-low.

### Seed 8: **ASHA Sawaal Line** (ASHABot, but on a phone call with Gnani ASR)
- **Situation:** 38.3% of ASHABot audio had transcription errors and ASHAs stopped using voice. §2 row 3.
- **Gnani:** Prisma noisy Hindi as the fix; Evon RAG over public ASHA handbook; escalation with context to ANM (Gnani's compare page stresses handoff-with-context).
- **Awards:** Noise Canceller, Real Deal.
- **Risk:** reads as a "support bot" (IndicVoiceCare niche) and as a Q&A product; weak visual.

### Seed 9: **Mazdoor Awaaz** (e-Shram registration by voice for domestic workers)
- **Situation:** 46% registered despite 79% awareness; language and procedure barriers; occupation codes missing. §2 row 16.
- **Gnani:** Prisma bn/hi, Evon occupation-code mapping, Timbre read-back.
- **Awards:** Real Deal, Main Character (Bengali/Bhojpuri-accented Hindi).
- **Risk:** "voice form-filling" already claimed by Bolo; e-Shram's own 22-language Bhashini layer; registration-without-benefit demo feels hollow.

### Seed 10: **FPO Kharid Call** (procurement calls → ledger)
- **Situation:** FPO CEOs with poor financial literacy, Simplykhata hand-holding. §2 row 11.
- **Gnani:** Prisma batch + ITN (quintals, ₹/qtl, dates) → ledger; essentially Seed 2 for FPOs.
- **Risk:** smaller, less visible constituency than SHGs; fold into Seed 2 as a second persona rather than a separate project.

### Seed 11: **Kilkari Do-Taraf** (two-way outbound maternal calls)
- **Situation:** missed outbound calls, 40% dropout. §2 row 5.
- **Gnani:** Timbre outbound + Prisma to capture "call me at 7 pm" and a symptom check; Evon triage.
- **Risk:** needs telephony (Twilio/Exotel) to be credible; outbound health calls are sensitive; weak 60 s visual.

### Seed 12: **KCC Overflow Agent** (voice agent fronting Kisan Call Centre)
- **Situation:** 1.69 lakh unanswered calls; govt wants AI; public KCC transcript dataset exists (OGL). §2 row 10.
- **Gnani:** Prisma 8 kHz + Evon RAG over KCC transcripts + handoff-with-context.
- **Risk:** most crowded idea in Indic AI (Farmer.Chat, Kisan Sarathi, Kisan e-Mitra, Bhashini VoicERA); judges have seen it.

### Why seeds 1-3 are the three to take forward [SPEC]
- **Evidence density:** each has 2024-26 primary sources with numbers (42,000 strikers; 10 Cr SHG members with a glitching app; a statutory 72-hour clock).
- **Gnani-shaped:** all three are *phone calls with bad audio and numbers in them*, which is the one place Prisma's 8 kHz/ITN/noisy-WER story is a genuine moat over Bhashini/Sarvam, and all three fit the Artha page's own "health follow-ups / scheme enrolment / disaster triage" examples.
- **Synthetic by construction:** Timbre voices + noise + AMR-NB produce the demo audio; no real person is recorded; fake IDs are harmless.
- **Visible in 60 s:** a table filling itself from a noisy call, a rupee total, a countdown.
- **Not taken:** none of the five public challenge repos touches incentive claims, diarized meetings or crop-loss intimation; the "audio-notes summariser" baseline is avoided because the output is a *structured record with a money/deadline consequence*, not a summary.
- **Interview story:** each one lets Harshdip talk about WER on AMR-NB, ITN edge cases (bigha vs acre, lakh), diarization DER, and deterministic slot validation (FormBharo), which are the skills Gnani's open roles list.
