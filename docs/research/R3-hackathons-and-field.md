## 1. Executive summary

1. **The field is tiny and early.** Four days in, the challenge has produced **5 identifiable public entries on GitHub** (Bolo, DriveOS, IndicVoiceCare, Samasya, boli-zero), **2 indexed entrant posts on LinkedIn** (IndicVoiceCare: 5 reactions; Voice Khata in Kannada: 26 reactions) and **one Lovable toy** (Meal Genie). Gnani's own launch post has 115 reactions / 7 comments on LinkedIn and **14 likes on X** ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gnani-ai_gnaniai-greatindianaiinternshipchallenge-activity-7511341641274884096-dHzG), [X syndication](https://cdn.syndication.twimg.com/tweet-result?id=2105582718222585919&token=a)). The "Crowd Favourite" bar is therefore low today; a post with a few hundred reactions would lead. The ~30 "audio notes" repos are a hiring take-home, not entries (see `06-…md`).
2. **Three idea spaces are already dead for a student entry:** Hinglish support/sales voice bots (Sarvam Voice Agents went GA at ₹3.50/min on 13 Aug 2026 after "350M conversations"; Ringg, Bolna, Navana, plus 5 student repos this week), scheme/FAQ chatbots (Jugalbandi 2023, Bhasha Techathon 2024, SIH 2024–26), and dubbing/live translation (Sarvam Dub dubbed the Union Budget live; Dubverse won Bhashini's live-translation PS; ElevenLabs Dubbing v2). Voice form-filling is **crowded** (Bolo in this contest, FormBharo paper, two IndiaAI 2026 problem statements, SIH26097) but still demanded by government.
3. **What wins Indic hackathons is not language count.** Across Warpspeed 2025, Bhashini's challenges, the Ken 2025 finale and the NHA 2026 hackathon, judges rewarded (a) a narrow, real workflow with a named user, (b) evidence of deployment or measured task completion, (c) the system around the model (validation, fallback, human handoff), and (d) demos that show a failure being handled. "Multilingual chatbot" wins nothing anymore.
4. **Live demand signals** cluster around: voice intake of structured records in noisy settings (OPD transcription for NIC eHospital, AYUSH voice EHR, MSME dispute filing, PM-AJAY livelihood interviews), **multi-speaker** speech (Sarvam's Indic DiarBench shows every Indic benchmark was single-speaker), **offline/edge** voice (Bhashini VYOMA ₹80 lakh), **voice-clone fraud and consent** (SIH26104; Delhi HC ruling; 20,000 dubbing artists), and **feature-phone/IVR** channels (Hello UPI on-call, SIH26097 "IVR for feature phones").
5. **Gnani's own stated open problems are unclaimed by any entrant:** three-way code-switching, prosodic switch cues, a WER benchmark on AMR-NB 5.9 kbps rural audio, Bhojpuri/Maithili/Chhattisgarhi WER (33.3% on Bhojpuri), and on-device personalisation ([code-switch paper](https://www.gnani.ai/resources/research/code-switching-at-8khz), [rural ASR paper](https://www.gnani.ai/resources/research/the-rural-india-asr-problem-engineering-speech-recognition-for-background-noise)). boli-zero touches Bhojpuri but has "no benchmark".
6. **Strongest seeds (§6):** **Garage Job-Card** (workshop-noise Hinglish to invoice; Gnani's auto vertical), **Sun-Saathi** (live regional-script captions on real phone calls for hard-of-hearing users, Timbre speaks replies), and **CodeMix Crash-Test** (a reproducible noisy-telephony code-switch test harness for your own voice agent, built around Gnani's missing-benchmark admission). Alternate: **Kendra Scribe** (SHG/JLG centre-meeting minutes to ledger, matching Gnani's CreditAccess Grameen customer).

## 5. Patterns in what wins (judging signals and demo styles)

Evidence base: Warpspeed 2025 (Lightspeed + Sarvam), Sarvam Epoch Buildathon 2026, Bhashini GIC/MHC/Techathon, Ken 2025 finale, NHA 2026, VYOMA criteria, Code for a Billion rubric, and Gnani's own blog.

1. **A named user doing one job beats a platform.** Warpspeed's grand prize went to DreamOps (on-call debugging triage, "30–60 minutes to 2–5 minutes"), not to any of the many multilingual chatbots; the recap says judges wanted "real-world deployment potential rather than weekend prototypes" ([Devfolio](https://devfolio.co/blog/warpspeed-2025-recap/), [Lightspeed](https://x.com/LightspeedIndia/status/1937084673496404199)). Ken 2025's winner converted "expensive hospital bed days into … AI-orchestrated home days" for one incumbent; judges praised understanding "the entire system of alternatives, incentives, risks, and dependencies" ([The Ken](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/the-ken_after-three-months-of-battle-indian-institute-activity-7407381828422074369-iPxl)).
2. **Numbers in the demo.** Government challenges write thresholds (Bhashini GIC: ">95% accuracy, <1 s latency, DMOS >4.2"); VYOMA names "language-level error rates, task completion rates"; Code for a Billion weights "proven impact: real deployment data" at 25%. The strongest non-Gnani student repos this week all ship a measurement: 156 simulated calls and a speech-under-noise test ([Voice-Sales-Agent](https://github.com/Khushal-Narsaria/Voice-Sales-Agent)), "v1 30% to v2 62% on a locked 11-check rubric" over 7 real calls ([bolna redteam](https://github.com/Aarti-panchal01/bolna-pizza-agent-redteam)), a CI gate for order correctness ([indicorderbench](https://github.com/Lingikaushikreddy/indicorderbench)). Gnani's blog: "honest limitations outperform faked results".
3. **Show the failure being handled.** FormBharo found end-to-end completion "drops by up to ~41 points" on real transcripts and that rule-based validation recovered it ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06027)); the Bolna red-team's headline is one bug ("haan, do do" doubled the order). A 60-second demo that includes one misrecognition and a graceful readback/handoff is more credible than a clean run.
4. **Noise and the phone are the differentiators that match the awards and the market.** Navana raised on "noisy environments and low-connectivity"; Gnani's Noise Canceller award says "messy, noisy, real telephonic audio"; no current entrant has demonstrated real 8 kHz call audio.
5. **Scale of the claim should match a solo build.** Winners in Bhashini's challenges were companies (Techgentsia, Dubverse, CoRover). Student winners (Warpspeed, Techathon) won on a tight scope with a working loop, not coverage of 22 languages.
6. **Demo style that travels on LinkedIn/X:** phone held to the camera, real environment audio, captions of what was said vs what was understood, one on-screen metric, a human in frame (shopkeeper, mechanic, grandmother). Gnani's Demo Day award is literally "best 60-second demo video"; its own B2C team hires reels editors (`06-…md` §1.4), so **[SPEC]** reel-grade pacing will be noticed.
7. **Sovereignty is a bonus narrative, not a differentiator among entrants.** Every entrant is on Gnani already; only a self-hosted Evon (vLLM) or an "audio never leaves the device until consented" story adds anything, and it maps to Gnani's LLMOps hiring.

### Strongest three

1. **S1 Garage Job-Card.** Matches a Gnani vertical, the Noise Canceller brief and the Real Deal brief at once, has a visceral reel, exercises all three models with amounts/IDs where Gnani claims an edge, and has zero collisions. Risk is manageable with a readback loop.
2. **S2 Sun-Saathi.** The only seed that demonstrates Prisma on *real* telephony audio, in an unserved segment, with an emotional 60-second story for Crowd Favourite. Engineering risk (call-audio capture) is the thing to de-risk in week one.
3. **S3 CodeMix Crash-Test.** The most defensible "developer tools" entry, built on Gnani's own admission that the benchmark does not exist; strong interview story for LLMOps/speech-data roles. Keep it a self-test harness to stay clear of the EULA benchmark clause. Pairs naturally with S1 or S2 as the evaluation half of the same submission (one award per team, so choose which to nominate).

**Alternate:** S4 Kendra Scribe if a CreditAccess-style field partner can be reached; otherwise its diarization risk is too high for a solo five-week build.

### What to avoid (restated from evidence)
Hinglish support/sales bots, kirana khata, Bhojpuri-through-Hindi, generic voice forms, meal/household agents, scheme chatbots, farmer IVR, dubbing/live translation, audio-notes summarisers, anything whose demo is a clean studio recording.
