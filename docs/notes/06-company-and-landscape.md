## TL;DR (decision-relevant)

1. **Gnani's identity is telephony voice AI for regulated Indian enterprises.** That means BFSI collections, insurance, auto, real estate and government, on noisy 8 kHz calls in code-mixed Indian languages, run on-prem or "sovereign". In 2026 they added an LLM (Evon) and a "sovereign stack" (Artha) for the IndiaAI and DPDP narrative. A project that looks like **a real call-centre or field workflow on bad phone audio**, with sovereignty and data residency built into the pitch, matches how they describe themselves. [about](https://www.gnani.ai/about-us), [Artha](https://www.gnani.ai/artha-sovereign-ai), [customer stories](https://www.gnani.ai/customer-stories)
2. **Field size: Discord "Gnani AI APIs" has 483 members, 27 online (2026-10-04).** On GitHub, about 44 repos matching "gnani" were created since 2026-09-25. Only a handful are explicitly challenge entries, and **most of the rest are an "audio notes" take-home** (upload audio, Gnani Batch STT, LLM summary), which looks like a separate Gnani hiring assignment **[SPEC]**. **Avoid anything that looks like an audio-notes summariser.** [Discord invite API](https://discord.com/api/v9/invites/aECcvtABpq?with_counts=true), [GitHub search](https://api.github.com/search/repositories?q=gnani+created:%3E2026-09-25)
3. **Public challenge entries so far:** Bolo (voice form-filling, Prisma+Timbre+Evon, Gemini stand-in), DriveOS (voice mission ops), IndicVoiceCare (Hinglish support agent), Samasya (voice puzzle games), **boli-zero (Bhojpuri through a Hindi stack, Claude as LLM)**. The "form-filling", "support bot" and "Bhojpuri" niches are already taken (see §3).
4. **Press framing of what to build** (CTO Ananth Nagaraj's launch release): apps "for enterprises, developers and creators", e.g. multilingual document processing, developer tools, regional-language content distribution. [PNI](https://www.pninews.com/gnani-ai-launches-the-great-indian-ai-internship-challenge/), [FintechBizNews](https://www.fintechbiznews.com/fintech-technology/great-indian-ai-internship-challenge-by-gnani-ai)
5. **What they hire for (careers.gnani.ai, 22 openings):** LLMOps (vLLM/SGLang/TensorRT-LLM), speech/language **data engineers**, full-stack + AI, DevOps. There is also a whole **"B2C Products"** department (mobile app engineer, growth/performance marketing, reels video editor, UI/UX). **[SPEC]** Gnani is building a consumer app, so creator- and consumer-facing builds plus social growth skills may be valued alongside infra skills. [careers](https://careers.gnani.ai/)
6. **Gnani's main competitor, Sarvam, now markets Saaras v3 as "optimized for 8KHz telephony" and code-mixed speech, in 23 languages.** Gnani's differentiation is therefore *data scale on real calls* (14M h), noisy-audio WER, domain normalisation and the on-prem/sovereign stack. It is **not** language count: Sarvam, AI4Bharat and Bhashini cover 22+ languages, while Gnani's API covers 10. [Sarvam docs](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/models/saaras), [Prisma microsite](https://gnani-prisma-v2-5-benchmarks.netlify.app/)
7. **Gnani is also the voice-rail partner in The Ken Case Competition 2026** (Zerodha title partner, with Pine Labs and Delhivery; household-agent theme; finale 10 Oct). Several of those teams are now also building on Gnani's APIs (e.g. `raahi-gnani-mcp`, `rasoi-household-agent`, `suljha`). [The Ken](https://the-ken.com/case-competition-2026/) (via search snippet; page returns 403)

### 3.1 How to make a project show off Gnani's strengths [SPEC]
- **Lean into:** (1) real phone or noisy audio (8 kHz streaming, `x-sample-rate 8000`, rural or field conditions), which is Prisma's measured home turf and the Noise Canceller award. (2) Hinglish/Tanglish code-mixing inside a domain (EMI, policy IDs, amounts), where Gnani claims a 17.9% switch-point MER and Timbre does domain normalisation for "₹45,000", card numbers and IDs. (3) **Sovereignty**: an open-weights Evon self-hosted, with "no data leaves" as part of the story, which matches the Artha and DPDP narrative. (4) Agentic voice that *does* something (outbound or follow-up loops, IVR-like flows), matching the Artha/Plexus use cases and the Ken brief. (5) Evon's real strength, **extracting structured answers from Indic text**, plus its token efficiency.
- **Avoid:** audio-notes summarisers (the take-home baseline), generic chatbots, anything needing Odia, Assamese or Bhojpuri ASR (not in the API), long-form summarisation with Evon (CrossSum 4.67), and headline claims that "Gnani beats X" with public numbers (EULA benchmark clause plus organiser sensitivities).
- **Gaps that are free for the taking:** Gnani's own list of open problems includes **3-way code-switching** (e.g. Hindi+English+Bhojpuri), prosodic switch cues, and Evon in a **non-reasoning real-time voice mode** ([code-switch paper](https://www.gnani.ai/resources/research/code-switching-at-8khz), [Evon FAQ](https://introducing-evon-v3-3-llm.netlify.app/)). Their rural-ASR paper describes **AMR-NB, 2G, AGC and low-end-mic degradation** without offering an end-user product ([rural paper](https://www.gnani.ai/resources/research/the-rural-india-asr-problem-engineering-speech-recognition-for-background-noise)). A field-worker or farmer tool on a feature-phone call path would fill that gap and also fits the Real Deal and Small Town awards.
