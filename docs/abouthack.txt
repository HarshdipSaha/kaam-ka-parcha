What you build on

Models trained in India, for Indian speech
Gnani AI builds its own speech and language models and trains them on real telephonic audio, so they handle noisy calls, code-mixed speech and Indian languages natively. Deployed together on infrastructure the customer controls, they form Artha, the sovereign stack. You get to build on the same models.

Speech to text
Prisma v2.5
Trained on 14M+ hours of telephonic audio. Streams in under 200ms across 10+ Indian languages plus Hinglish, Tanglish and Benglish, according to Gnani AI's published benchmarks.

Speech to text API
Text to speech, Beta
Timbre v2.5
Speaks 10+ Indic languages with context-aware prosody, Indian names, numerals and lakh and crore cadence. Streams over WebSocket and SSE.

Text to speech API
Reasoning, open weight
Evon v3.3
A 30B parameter model with about 3.5B active per token, 128K context and 11 Indian languages natively. Apache 2.0 weights are available by request on Hugging Face.

Eligibility

Who can apply
The programme is open to students. Check all three conditions before you register.

You must be
✓
Currently enrolled in a degree or course at a college or university in India.
✓
18 years or older on the day you register.
✓
Building solo, or with one teammate who also meets these conditions.
Solo or a team of two
Solo
You register, build and submit on your own.

Team of two
You register first, then invite one teammate. Teams can't be larger than two.

Your teammate accepts the terms independently and their email is kept on record only for verification and testing, while all contest communication goes to the primary registrant, and each of you is interviewed and considered for an offer individually.

Read before you register

The rules that matter most
These points shape who gets an offer. The full terms appear on the registration page, and every participant accepts them individually.

1
Stipends are per person
The stipend of up to ₹1 lakh a month is paid to each intern individually. It is not split across a team.

2
Offers are individual
Teammates are interviewed separately. One member can receive an offer without the other.

3
Your project uses Gnani AI technology
Your submission names the Gnani AI models or APIs it uses and each member's contribution.

4
You demo it in public
Post your project on LinkedIn or X with the hashtags #GnaniAI and #GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge, then nominate those posts so Gnani AI can verify their engagement.

5
The deadline is fixed
Final submissions close on November 10, 2026. You can edit project details until you submit.

6
Only written offers count
An internship is confirmed only by a written offer from Gnani AI after the interview stage.

Gnani AI may modify, suspend or cancel any part of the contest, including rules, timelines and prizes, as set out in the programme terms.
